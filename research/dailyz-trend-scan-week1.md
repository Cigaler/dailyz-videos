## 2. Three urgent video ideas to produce immediately

### Idea 1: Google's AI video stack in plain English

- Topic: `Veo 3` + `Flow`
- Hook line: `Google just made AI video way harder to ignore. Here’s the 30-second breakdown.`
- Why now: this is the clearest spike in creator chatter right now and it is highly visual
- Format:
  - show `Veo 3` output first
  - explain what `Flow` changes for editing
  - end with one sentence on who should care: creators, freelancers, agencies

### Idea 2: The new coding stack everyone is arguing about

- Topic: `Cursor` vs `Replit` vs `Claude Code`
- Hook line: `Everyone is debating Cursor, Replit, and Claude Code. Here’s the stack I’d actually pick today.`
- Why now: this is a live "which tool wins?" conversation, not a stale tutorial category
- Format:
  - one sentence per tool
  - one winner for speed
  - one winner for non-technical users
  - one final recommendation

### Idea 3: The AI agent test that feels real

- Topic: `AI agents` + `n8n`
- Hook line: `If your AI agent can’t finish one real task, it’s not an agent.`
- Why now: skeptical, proof-first framing is outperforming vague "future of AI" claims
- Format:
  - name one task: inbox cleanup, lead sorting, or content repurposing
  - show the trigger
  - show the finished output
  - end with `this is why n8n is everywhere this week`
