## 2. Three urgent video ideas to produce immediately

### Idea 1: Claude Code vs Codex vs Jules in one sentence each

- Hook line: `Everyone is comparing Claude Code, Codex, and Jules. Here’s the only 30-second ranking that actually matters.`
- Why it is urgent:
  - `Claude Code` is clearly hot now.
  - `Codex` is entering the conversation fast.
  - comparison content consistently outperforms generic tutorials in this niche.
- Format:
  - open with the three logos in the first second
  - one line each: best for speed, best for non-technical users, best for serious workflows
  - close with one blunt recommendation
- Estimated virality: `9/10`

### Idea 2: Google just made AI video creation easier than people realize

- Hook line: `Google quietly made AI video creation way easier, and most creators still haven’t caught up.`
- Why it is urgent:
  - `#googleveo3` is still the strongest short-term visual spike
  - `Flow` is now shifting from novelty into practical creator workflow
- Format:
  - show one Veo/Flow output immediately
  - explain what changed in plain English
  - close with “who should use this today”
- Estimated virality: `8.5/10`

### Idea 3: The first AI agent setup that feels real, not fake

- Hook line: `If your AI agent can’t finish one real task, it’s not an agent.`
- Why it is urgent:
  - `AI agents`, `n8n`, and `MCP` are moving together now
  - skeptical framing matches the current mood better than futuristic hype
- Format:
  - one task only: research summary, lead sorting, or content repurposing
  - show the trigger
  - show the final output
  - name the stack in the last line: `Claude Code + n8n + MCP`
- Estimated virality: `8/10`

## 4. Comparison with week 1

### Still hot

- `Veo 3` + `Flow`: still the number-one visual opportunity. It did not cool off.
- `Claude Code`: hotter than week 1 because more creators are now attaching outcomes to it.
- `vibe coding`: still strong and arguably broader than week 1.
- `AI agents` + `n8n`: still moving, but the audience now wants proof, not theory.
- `NotebookLM`: still reliable, especially for Gen-Z / study / research angles.

### Cooling off

- `Manus AI` / browser-agent demos: still usable, but no longer feels like the main conversation.
- generic `ChatGPT prompts` content: still works, but it is no longer the sharpest “new” hook.
- `Perplexity` / “stop using Google” answer-engine content: less urgent this week than agentic coding and AI-video creation. The public [`#perplexityai`](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/perplexityai) tag was only around `6,233 posts` in this check, which is materially smaller than the hotter week-2 tags.

### Surprises

- `OpenAI Codex` showed up much faster as a creator topic than I expected. It is not huge on TikTok yet, but it is clearly entering comparison content.
- `MCP` became more creator-visible this week. In week 1 it was mostly background infrastructure; in week 2 it is part of the actual content hook.
- `Google Stitch` held longer than expected. Prompt-to-UI demos are still visually sticky.
