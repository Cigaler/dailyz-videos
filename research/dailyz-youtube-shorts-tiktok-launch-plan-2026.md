# DailyZ YouTube Shorts + TikTok Launch Plan

## Scope

This document is a ready-to-execute launch plan for DailyZ's YouTube Shorts and TikTok channels.

Assumptions used:

- zero paid ads
- AI-generated content only
- no face or camera
- goal is speed to monetization without using spammy or recycled content
- target market is US Gen Z / young millennial trend consumers
- all posting times below are in US Eastern Time

## Recommended launch position

DailyZ should launch as:

- a daily trend explainer account
- fast, searchable, voiceover-led, faceless videos
- focused on "what is blowing up, why it matters, and what happens next"

This keeps the niche broad enough to trend-surf while still making the brand legible.

### Brand direction

- Tone: fast, sharp, curious, slightly skeptical
- Promise: "daily internet trend context in under 60 seconds"
- Visual style: bold text, hard contrast, minimal clutter

- Secondary color: `#101010` near-black

### Banner / header line

- Secondary line: `Shorts + TikToks every day`

## 4. Hashtag strategy

## Core rule

Hashtags help more on TikTok than on YouTube. On YouTube, title, thumbnail, and description matter more than tags. Do not stuff hashtags.

## YouTube Shorts hashtag stack

Use `2 to 3` hashtags max.

Default formula:

- `#shorts`
- one topic hashtag
- one branded hashtag

Examples:

- `#shorts #internettrends #dailyz`
- `#shorts #viralapps #dailyz`
- `#shorts #memeculture #dailyz`

Do not add long strings of hashtags in the description.

## TikTok hashtag stack

Use `4 to 6` hashtags per post.

Default formula:

- `1` broad discovery tag
- `2` trend/topic tags
- `1` search-intent tag
- `1` branded tag
- `1` rotating format tag if relevant

Recommended always-on TikTok tags:

- `#dailyz`
- `#internettrends`
- `#explained`

Rotating niche tags:

- `#memes`
- `#viralapps`
- `#tiktoknews`
- `#internetculture`
- `#popculture`
- `#creatornews`
- `#trendalert`
- `#genz`
- `#whatsgoingon`
- `#explained`

## Trending vs niche mix

- `20%` broad tags
- `60%` niche / search-intent tags
- `20%` branded or recurring-series tags

## Hashtag rules

- Never use irrelevant trending hashtags just because they are large
- On TikTok, pair the hashtag set with on-screen keyword text and spoken keywords
- On YouTube, prioritize searchable titles and first-line descriptions over tag stuffing

## 7. Growth hacks for first 500 YouTube subs and 10K TikTok followers

These are legitimate speed tactics, not spam tactics.

1. Launch with volume, not perfection.
   - Start with a bank of `20 to 30` finished videos before the first post.
   - Publish daily for 30 straight days with no gaps.

2. Build only 3 repeatable series.
   - `Why this is trending`
   - `What happened in 30 seconds`
   - `The internet is divided on this`
   This makes the brand recognizable fast.

3. Keep the first 2 seconds brutally clear.
   - Hook must answer one of these:
   - what is this
   - why is everyone talking about it
   - why should I care

4. Make one TikTok per day at `60 to 90` seconds.
   - This keeps the Creator Rewards path alive because eligible videos must be original and longer than one minute.

5. Cut a second, shorter version for YouTube Shorts.
   - Same topic, tighter edit, different export, different captioning.

6. Use search-led topic sourcing every morning.
   - TikTok Creator Search Insights
   - TikTok Discover / Search
   - YouTube Trends tab
   - YouTube Trending Charts
   - Google Trends

7. Follow and study adjacent accounts immediately.
   - Follow `25 to 40` accounts in internet culture, memes, creator news, and viral products.
   - Save hooks, caption styles, and recurring topics.

8. Comment strategically from the founder account.
   - Leave smart, non-spam comments on large adjacent creators within the niche.
   - Goal is profile curiosity, not link dropping.

9. Reply to comments with videos fast.
   - On TikTok, turn good comments into follow-up posts.
   - On YouTube, pin a question under every Short and use answers as next-video prompts.

10. Use a daily scoreboard.
   - Track every post for:
   - retention
   - shares
   - comments
   - profile visits
   - follows or subs
   Double down only on the top `20%` performers.

11. Optimize for shares, not likes.
   - Trend explanation content spreads when it makes viewers feel informed first.
   - End with share-worthy framing such as `send this to the friend who keeps seeing this online`.

12. Post around fresh trends, not dead trends.
   - The sweet spot is `6 to 24` hours after a topic starts visibly rising.
   - Too early has no search demand.
   - Too late is crowded.

13. Use consistent visual branding.
   - Same caption style
   - Same opener structure
   - Same avatar and color system
   This improves recognition when viewers see multiple videos in one week.

14. Avoid the fastest way to get suppressed.
   - no stolen clips
   - no reused watermarked exports
   - no low-effort slideshow spam
   - no repetitive near-duplicate uploads
   - no fake engagement

15. Set a 14-day experiment cadence.
   - Days 1 to 14: test multiple hooks and lengths
   - Days 15 to 30: narrow to the best 2 formats and scale those
